Straight Answers

BEFORE YOU BOOK.

How long does a garage floor installation take?

Many standard garage floor systems can be installed in one day depending on slab condition, repairs, weather and the selected system. We'll confirm the exact schedule before work begins.

Why do you diamond grind the concrete?

Mechanical grinding creates the surface profile needed for strong adhesion and removes weak surface material, contaminants and old coatings that can cause failures.

Is this the same as a DIY epoxy kit?

No. The system, preparation equipment, repair process, application method and professional topcoat are built for a very different level of durability than a roll-on DIY kit.

Will hot tires damage the coating?

The system is designed for normal garage vehicle traffic and includes a durable topcoat intended for real-world garage use.

Is the calculator a final quote?

No. It gives you a useful ballpark range based on the project details you enter. Final pricing is confirmed after we measure the space and inspect the concrete in person.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. Qualifying OCC coating systems include a written lifetime warranty. We'll review the warranty details with you before installation.
